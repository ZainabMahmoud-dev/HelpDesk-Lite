# HelpDesk Lite — One Iteration Improvement

## Weak Part Identified

During the review of the project communication, the ticket workflow and stakeholder decision points were identified as areas that could be clearer.

The initial communication described the workflow, but the relationship between each workflow step and the stakeholder decisions was not explicit enough.

## What Changed

The workflow was refined into a clear sequence:

**Create → Assign → Prioritize → Work → Resolve → Close**

The communication was also updated to make the required stakeholder decisions explicit:

- Confirm the proposed workflow.
- Confirm the initial ticket statuses.
- Define how ticket priority should be handled.
- Confirm ownership and assignment responsibilities.
- Confirm the initial implementation scope.

## Why This Is Stronger

The improvement makes the project easier to review because stakeholders can quickly understand:

1. How a ticket moves through the system.
2. Which parts of the workflow require confirmation.
3. What decisions need to be made before implementation continues.

The change also reduces ambiguity and keeps the communication focused on the project's actual objective rather than unnecessary technical or visual details.

## Evidence of Iteration

The improvement is reflected in the following project documents:

- `ai-prompt-first-output.md` — First AI-generated version
- `review-improved-version.md` — Review findings and improvements
- `final-stakeholder-brief.md` — Final refined communication

The final version is therefore the result of an explicit review and iteration process rather than direct acceptance of the first AI output.
