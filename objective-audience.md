# HelpDesk Lite — Objective and Audience Definition

## Communication Objective

The objective is to communicate the HelpDesk Lite solution clearly to stakeholders by explaining the current support challenges, the proposed centralized ticketing workflow, and the expected business value.

The communication should help stakeholders understand how HelpDesk Lite improves ticket ownership, follow-up, visibility, and support management.

## Target Audience

### Primary Audience

- Product Owner / Project Owner
- IT Support Manager
- Business Stakeholders

### Secondary Audience

- IT Support Team
- Development Team

## Decision or Action Supported

The deliverable should support stakeholders in:

- Confirming whether the proposed HelpDesk Lite workflow addresses the current support problems.
- Approving the initial ticket management scope.
- Confirming ticket statuses, priority rules, and ownership responsibilities.
- Providing focused feedback before implementation continues.

## What the Audience Needs to Understand

The audience needs to understand:

1. The main problems with the current support request process.
2. How HelpDesk Lite addresses these problems.
3. How a support request moves from creation to resolution and closure.
4. The expected benefits for support teams, users, and managers.
5. Which decisions and confirmations are required from stakeholders.
