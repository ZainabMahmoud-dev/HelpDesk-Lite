# HelpDesk Lite — Final Delivery

## Project Overview

HelpDesk Lite is an internal support ticketing workspace designed to centralize support requests and improve ownership, follow-up, and visibility.

## Problem

Internal support requests can be received through multiple channels, which can result in unclear ownership, inconsistent follow-up, repeated clarification, and limited visibility into ticket progress.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Ticket creation
- Ticket management
- Ticket ownership
- Ticket priority
- Ticket status tracking
- Ticket progress tracking

### Out of Scope

- Advanced automation
- External customer support
- Complex analytics
- Third-party integrations
- Features outside the initial internal support workflow

## Key Workflow

**Create → Assign → Prioritize → Work → Resolve → Close**

The workflow provides a consistent path for managing a support request from creation through closure.

## Key Decisions

- Centralize support requests into one workspace.
- Make ticket ownership explicit.
- Use priority and status to support follow-up.
- Keep the initial scope focused on core ticket management.
- Prioritize clarity and usability over unnecessary features.

Advanced automation, external customer support, complex analytics, and third-party integrations are outside the initial scope.

These limitations are intentionally documented to keep the delivery scope clear.
