# HelpDesk Lite — Stakeholder Decision Brief

## Purpose

HelpDesk Lite is a centralized internal support ticketing workspace designed to improve ownership, follow-up, and visibility across support requests.

---

## The Problem

Internal support requests are currently handled through multiple channels.

This creates:

- Unclear ownership
- Inconsistent follow-up
- Repeated clarification
- Limited visibility into open, delayed, and completed requests

---

## The Proposed Solution

HelpDesk Lite centralizes internal support requests into a single ticketing workspace.

Each request becomes a trackable ticket with:

- Clear ownership
- Priority
- Current status
- Progress tracking

This creates a consistent process from request creation through resolution and closure.
